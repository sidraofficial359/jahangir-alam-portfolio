# Jahangir Alam - Portfolio Website

A modern, dark-themed personal portfolio showcasing development, automation, and AI expertise, featuring an interactive chatbot assistant.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Futuristic Dark UI:** Sleek glassmorphism effects with neon pink and purple accents.
* **Interactive AI Assistant:** "Jahangir's AI" chatbot to answer visitor queries, discuss projects, and provide contact details.
* **Responsive Sections:** Includes Home, About Me, Skill Cards, Projects, and a direct Contact form.

---

## 🛠️ Areas of Expertise

* **AI Master & Automator:** Intelligent workflows, model integration, and automation systems.
* **Web & App Development:** Modern responsive frontend design and application building.
* **Excel Expert:** Advanced data tools and workflow optimization.

---
